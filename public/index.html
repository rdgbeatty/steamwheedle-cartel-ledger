Metrics & data notes

    Profit × volume = (Horde ask × 0.95 × 0.85 − Alliance cost) × the smaller faction volume. This is a maximum opportunity score: the neutral price is set to the Horde buyer's break-even price, leaving them zero profit. Click ⓘ for both fees and both traders' profits at the actual neutral minimum. Your desired minimum profit is a filter on this net spread; it is never added to profit. Full Alliance stock is not all available at its minimum ask. Neutral liquidity, deposits, relisting and price changes are excluded; this score is not a buy quantity or expected realized profit.

    Filter low volume starts enabled, requiring 1,000 units on both faction markets. Change the threshold and both/either matching in Trade filters. Unchecking it removes the volume minimum while retaining the profit filters and score multiplier. The score uses the smaller faction volume. Listed stock measures supply. Where both daily estimates exist, the default uses estimated units/day. Daily-only mode excludes missing coverage and measured zero stays zero. Each score shows its basis.

    Sales estimates from AHledger ↗ · Scan-based methodology ↗. Estimates may confuse cancellations with sales and may be unavailable. Only Forever US PvP Alliance/Horde are used.

    Expected profit = Σ (Alliance minimum ask × quantity × 0.95 − neutral purchase cost), for profitable neutral price levels only. Click ⓘ to inspect quantities, costs, the 5% deduction and totals. Profit is shown in gold with 3 significant figures (k = thousand, M = million, B = billion, T = trillion); sorting uses full values. The model assumes every purchased unit sells at the current Alliance minimum. It excludes deposits, relisting costs and price changes. The fee is modeled as exactly 5%, without rounding each sale's fee. Stock measures listed supply, not completed sales.

    Prices are buyouts per item. Weighted medians use buyout quantities. Stock and offer counts describe listed supply; bid-only offers are counted separately and excluded from buyout prices and stock. Uncheck “Only listed items” to include catalog items with no current listings. Unavailable means a market could not be read; Not listed means it was read successfully with no buyout. Missing item metadata appears as Item #ID / Unknown.
