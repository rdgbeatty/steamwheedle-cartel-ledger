Privacy and retention

Gathering Ledger displays public US PvP auction prices from Blizzard Entertainment. The collector discards raw auction responses after processing. The current market snapshot includes item metadata and anonymous quotes and expires within 30 days of its oldest source. Anonymous price and stock history is available for up to 90 days from its source.

The public market app requires no sign-in and uses no advertising trackers. GitHub Pages hosts the interface, and Cloudflare receives your IP address when serving prices and charts. Market data and the archive contain no player names, account identifiers or auction IDs.
