Privacy and retention

This free viewer requests public US PvP auction prices from Blizzard. Anonymous prices, quantities and listing counts are available for up to 90 days from their source. The collector discards raw auction responses after processing.

The viewer uses no sign-in, cookies or advertising trackers. Cloudflare receives your IP address and requested item when serving a chart. The application stores market observations and collection status. It excludes player names, account identifiers and auction IDs from its archive.
